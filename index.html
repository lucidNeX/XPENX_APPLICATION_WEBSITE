Getting started

Is setup difficult? No — two minutes, three steps.

  1. 1

    Set up your budgets

    Go to Budget. Set your monthly limit, today's daily limit and a budget for each category such as Food, Travel or Bills.

  2. 2

    Customise your categories

    Open Categories to create, edit or delete them so they match your spending habits and show you where money goes.

  3. 3

    Add your utility bills

    Go to Bills › Add Utility, enter the name and amount, pick a category, and mark it as paid when it is done.

Questions

Good to know

How do I install the app?

Tap Download application, open the downloaded xpenx.apk file and allow installs from your browser or file manager if Android asks. Then launch XPENX.

Is it hard to set up?

No. Set a monthly budget, adjust your categories and add your first bill — that is all it takes, in under two minutes. A user guide is built into the app for new users.

What do the budgets do?

The monthly budget caps your overall spending, category budgets cap each category, and the daily budget carries forward each day until you change it. XPENX shows what is remaining at each level.

Can I move to a new phone?

Yes. Use Export › Backup to save your data and Restore on the new device or after reinstalling.

Which report formats are supported?

PDF, Excel (.xlsx) and CSV, for any range of months you choose.
